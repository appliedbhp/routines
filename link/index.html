Link accounts

Choose what you share with another account.

Sign in or create an account ↗

The other person can see your account email. Nothing else is shared until you select individual boards, goals, dates, or values in Settings. Each account uses one link slot.

Linking does not share private boards or self-monitoring results. One linked-account replacement is allowed per calendar month (Pacific Time). You can unlink in Settings → Account at any time. Copies someone has already printed or exported cannot be recalled.
