The simple details

  • Confirm your email before December 1, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time. Family access starts when you confirm. Classroom access starts after educator approval.
  • Beta access includes the selected Family or Classroom plan. Referral months extend your free access. Plus plans are coming soon and are not included or available to purchase yet.
  • Each new friend must confirm through your invitation link before the signup deadline. Each person can count once; self-referrals and duplicate accounts do not count.
  • Lifetime means the lifetime of the Routines service, at the plan purchased. Buying a lifetime plan does not shorten your remaining beta access.
  • No payment is required. When free access ends, your account returns to the free plan (2 cloud boards) or your purchased plan. Extra saved boards remain available to open, export, or delete; saving is limited to your current plan.
  • Cloud board copies contain settings only. Do not include names, classrooms, personal health information, or other identifying details. Session results stay on your device.

We store your signup email, confirmation and referral records, purchases, and any bug reports you send. Stripe handles payments; we don’t store card details. Friend addresses are used for the invitations you request, not added to a marketing list. Privacy details.
