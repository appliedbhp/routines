How to make a routine timer

  1. Choose Morning, Classroom, or Bedtime, or add custom steps in the builder.
  2. Set a duration and picture symbol for each activity. Choose a theme and adjust text or icon sizes.
  3. Open Projection for a live timer, or Print Preview to print the routine wheel or save it as a PDF.
  4. Save in this browser, or export a routine file to share with another parent or teacher.

Ways to use your visual timer

At home: start with getting dressed, breakfast, and brushing teeth. For bedtime, try a bath, pajamas, a book, and settling down. Adjust the minutes to fit your household.

In the classroom: project the wheel for circle time, reading, writing, cleanup, and recess. Use quiet mode for silent transitions or choose individual sound cues.

For flexible days: turn on Flexible schedule. The timer holds on the current activity until you press Next step, even if the planned time has elapsed.

Frequently asked questions

Is this visual routine timer free?

Yes. You can build, project, print, save, and export routines without an account. The printable page includes an optional affiliate link.

Can I print a visual routine?

Yes. Open Print Preview and choose Print / Save as PDF. Use US Letter paper at 100% or Actual size, with browser headers and footers turned off. The outer circle is 7 inches across and the center is 3.5 inches across.

Can I use the timer without sound or text?

Quiet mode is on by default. You can turn individual sounds on when you want them. Choose No text in either view for picture-only step labels, and hide the clock in Projection if you prefer.

Where are my saved routines stored?

Saved routines stay in this browser on this device. Export a JSON routine file for a backup or to move a routine to another device. Importing opens the file; press Save to keep it in that browser.

Does it work offline?

The timer runs in your browser, but picture searches, fonts, and QR images need internet access when they are not already cached.
