Teach homework skills together

Choose one skill to practice this week. Use the same cue at school and at home, then give the student more time to do the step independently as it gets easier.

  1. At school, model how to record it. Post the assignment and due date. Have the student write it on the blank copy or check None or Not posted yet. Agree on a cue like “Check each subject before you pack up.”
  2. At home, let the student check first. Scan the QR code together and compare the student’s copy with the teacher’s chart. Ask “What matches?” or “What do we need to check?” Mark Verified after comparing.
  3. Pick a next step and check in. Ask the student to choose what comes first and gather the materials. At the end of the week, share one thing they did independently and one cue that helped. Use that to choose the next skill.
