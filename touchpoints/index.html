When people feel “out of sight, out of mind”

If ADHD makes it hard to remember to reach out when someone is not in front of you, use this as a visible cue—not a measure of how much you care.

  • Start with a few important people and one meaningful date for each.
  • Pair a short check-in with something you already do: “After Sunday breakfast, I’ll message one person.”
  • Keep this list where you plan your week. Use a calendar reminder for time-sensitive events.
  • A small hello counts. If you miss a date, reconnect without waiting for the perfect message.
