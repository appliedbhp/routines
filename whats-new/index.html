What's New

New tools and small improvements to help everyday routines run more smoothly. Check back here as we add features.

October 8, 2026

Take your tools offline

Open the site online once and wait for “Available offline.” Download a board and its pictures for use without internet, then reopen it with “Open offline copy.” Install Visual Tools from your browser or add it to your iPad Home Screen. Cloud accounts and new picture searches need internet. Download again after edits, and export important boards as backups.

October 8, 2026

Two free cloud boards

Create a free adult account to save two reusable boards total across all visual tools and open them across devices. Choose the cloud icon in the top-right corner or visit My cloud boards. Review your board and confirm that it contains no personal information before each cloud save. Cloud copies contain settings only; session results stay on your device. Local saving and exports remain available.

October 8, 2026

Nine new self-monitor themes

Choose Classroom, Autumn, Halloween, Winter, Tropical, 80s, Valentine, St. Patrick’s, or City. Each projection theme includes matching point icons and a distinct background, with gentle motion where appropriate and reduced-motion support.

October 8, 2026

Four more self-monitor themes

Try Garden with flowers and drifting petals, Unicorn with pastel clouds and unicorns, Chef with warm café colors and burgers, or Scientist with rising bubbles and beakers. Projection effects respect reduced-motion settings.

October 8, 2026

A new self-monitor timer

Use the Self-monitor timer for work sessions up to 90 minutes. Choose equal or random check-ins, a custom question, and points for student/adult ratings. Smile icons show full and half points. Ocean now has gentle waves in projection mode. Forest clouds use filled Material Cloud icons, Space stars twinkle independently, and optional chimes distinguish student and adult prompts. Sessions now survive navigation and reloads in the same tab, preserving responses and stopping at the next unanswered check-in. Project full screen, choose animated projection scenes with themed point icons or a custom-color Classic theme, hide only the timer numbers, save or share setups, and print a student check-in card or an exit ticket with the period’s responses and points.

October 8, 2026

Plan your homework week

The new Homework planner offers simple Yes/No boxes or detailed daily assignments with Done checkboxes. Choose a weekly or single-day view, choose ARASAAC subject pictures, include weekends, and save, share, or print your plan in portrait or landscape with a QR code back to the site. New planners start with a blank single day. Printed weekday headings include dates when a week is selected, single-day printouts include gray writing grids, and the Reading, Math, and Writing example is ready to load. Switching versions preserves both sets of entries.

October 8, 2026

Fullscreen sessions for every board

Use Run session on First–Then, Task Strip, Now–Next–Later, Choice, Calm-down, and Chore Chart pages. Step-by-step boards unlock one step at a time; choice boards let you select any option. Chore sessions show one day at a time. Reset and Exit stay available, and progress carries back to the editor for saving.

October 8, 2026

Ready-made animated token boards

Start with Space, Critters, Clay — Games, or Clay — Relax in the token board’s Example menu. Each starts with zero tokens earned. Confetti now matches your theme, 12 more Google Fonts expand your choices, and softer musical chimes celebrate tokens and completed boards. Try the new Preview sound button.

October 8, 2026

More pictures, easier searching

Token boards now include PixaBots pixel characters, with animation during sessions and still pictures for printing. Search any activity or object across ARASAAC, Material Symbols, and OpenMoji together, regardless of the browse category.

October 8, 2026

Token-board sessions come to life

Run your token board fullscreen, celebrate each earned token with confetti, and enjoy a bigger celebration when the board is complete. Choose Classic, Ocean, Space, Garden, Arcade, or Boho to set the fonts and colors.

Explore 12 DiceBear styles, including planets, adventurers, critters, clay characters, and voxel art. Styles with built-in animation move during sessions; printed boards stay still. Turn off animations and confetti whenever you prefer.

October 8, 2026

A token board that feels like yours

Build a personalized token board with 1–12 tokens and a chosen reward. Mix activity pictures from ARASAAC, Material Symbols, colorful OpenMoji emoji, and DiceBear robot characters generated on your device.

Tap to award tokens during a session, optionally turn on celebration sounds, or print the board with separate cut-out token sets. Save progress, export a backup, and share a board with import instructions. Picture credits travel with your printouts.

October 8, 2026

Share your visuals and choose their layout

  • Share by email: prepare the file and step-by-step import, save, and load instructions. Use your device’s share menu, download an email with the attachment included, or attach the file to a prefilled email.
  • Rows, columns, or Auto: adjust board layouts. Auto arranges cards for one printed US Letter page. Layout choices travel with saved and shared files.
  • Get Ready with Me: a new nine-step task-strip example, with pictures ready to use.
  • Next step: skip forward in timed or flexible timer projection, including fullscreen. The border time markers update immediately.

Explore the routine timer, chore chart, First–Then board, choice board, task strip, Now–Next–Later board, and calm-down board.
