What's New

New tools and small improvements to help everyday routines run more smoothly. Check back here as we add features.

October 8, 2026

More pictures, easier searching

Token boards now include PixaBots pixel characters, with animation during sessions and still pictures for printing. Search any activity or object across ARASAAC, Material Symbols, and OpenMoji together, regardless of the browse category.

October 8, 2026

Token-board sessions come to life

Run your token board fullscreen, celebrate each earned token with confetti, and enjoy a bigger celebration when the board is complete. Choose Classic, Ocean, Space, Garden, Arcade, or Boho to set the fonts and colors.

Explore 12 DiceBear styles, including planets, adventurers, critters, clay characters, and voxel art. Styles with built-in animation move during sessions; printed boards stay still. Turn off animations and confetti whenever you prefer.

October 8, 2026

A token board that feels like yours

Build a personalized token board with 1–12 tokens and a chosen reward. Mix activity pictures from ARASAAC, Material Symbols, colorful OpenMoji emoji, and DiceBear robot characters generated on your device.

Tap to award tokens during a session, optionally turn on celebration sounds, or print the board with separate cut-out token sets. Save progress, export a backup, and share a board with import instructions. Picture credits travel with your printouts.

October 8, 2026

Share your visuals and choose their layout

  • Share by email: prepare the file and step-by-step import, save, and load instructions. Use your device’s share menu, download an email with the attachment included, or attach the file to a prefilled email.
  • Rows, columns, or Auto: adjust board layouts. Auto arranges cards for one printed US Letter page. Layout choices travel with saved and shared files.
  • Get Ready with Me: a new nine-step task-strip example, with pictures ready to use.
  • Next step: skip forward in timed or flexible timer projection, including fullscreen. The border time markers update immediately.
October 8, 2026

A familiar home for every visual

Switch tools from the collapsible left navigation. Editing options, save/share controls, and print actions sit across the top. The sidebar remembers your preference.

Explore the routine timer, chore chart, First–Then board, choice board, task strip, Now–Next–Later board, and calm-down board.
