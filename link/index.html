Link accounts

Share selected dates and values with your family or classroom.

Sign in or create an account ↗

Members can see your account email and the dates and values you choose to share with this group. Shared dates appear in members’ planners. Family or classroom values appear alongside personal values.

Your private boards and self-monitoring results are not shared by linking. You can unlink in Settings → Account at any time. Copies someone has already printed or exported cannot be recalled.
