Calm-down choice board

Choose supportive options together when things are calm. When your child wants support, offer the board and let them choose what feels helpful.

Edit the labels and click pictures to choose symbols. Save keeps this board in your browser. Export creates a file you can share or import on another device. Using an example or loading a board replaces the open cards; save your work first.
