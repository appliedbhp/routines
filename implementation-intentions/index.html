Let your location remind you

A small nudge at the right moment: “When I get to work, I will stretch for 10 minutes.”

↓ Download location shortcut

Opens Apple Shortcuts. Set up your reminder on your iPhone or iPad.

Set up an arrival reminder
  1. Add the shortcut. Open the link on your Apple device, review the shortcut, and choose Add Shortcut. Follow any setup prompts and enter your intention.
  2. Choose your place. In Shortcuts, open Automation and add an automation. Choose Arrive, then select your location, such as Work. Adjust the arrival area if needed.
  3. Connect your intention. Choose Intention 2, the shortcut you just added (or its new name if you renamed it). If your version shows an action editor, add Run Shortcut and select it. Choose Run Immediately if available.
  4. Check the reminder. Make sure the shortcut’s notification text says what you want to do—for example, “I will stretch for 10 minutes.” Run the shortcut once to test the message and review any permission prompts.

Adding the shortcut alone does not set an arrival location. The location trigger is configured in Apple Shortcuts on your device; Routines does not receive your location. Menu wording may vary by iOS or iPadOS version.

Let the time remind you

Make room for a small action: “When it is 8 PM, I will plan one priority for tomorrow.”

↓ Download time shortcut

Opens Apple Shortcuts. Set up your reminder on your iPhone or iPad.

Set up a time-based reminder
  1. Add the shortcut. Open the link on your Apple device, review Intention Time, and choose Add Shortcut. Follow any setup prompts and enter your intention.
  2. Choose when. In Shortcuts, open Automation, add an automation, and choose Time of Day. Set the time and repeat schedule: Daily, Weekly for selected days, or Monthly.
  3. Connect your intention. Select Intention Time (or its new name if you renamed it). If you see an action editor, add Run Shortcut and select it. Choose Run Immediately if available.
  4. Try your reminder. Check the notification text, run the shortcut once, and review any permission prompts. Choose a time when the action is realistic for you.

Adding the shortcut alone does not schedule it. Set the time trigger in Apple Shortcuts on your device. Menu wording may vary by iOS or iPadOS version.
